Asymmetric Financial Loss Function & Decision Matrix

In banking and fintech risk operations, standard symmetric metrics (Accuracy, ROC-AUC) hide critical financial dynamics. Failing to stop a fraudulent charge results in chargeback write-offs and dispute fees (-US$ 180), whereas falsely declining a customer's card incurs operational customer support costs (-US$ 30).

Net Utility = (120 × True Positives) - (30 × False Positives) - (180 × False Negatives) + (0 × True Negatives)
Asymmetric Loss Penalty Ratio = Cost(FN) / Cost(FP) = 180 / 30 = 6 : 1

Behavioral Profiling & Empirical Risk Drivers (EDA)

Exploration of 1,000,000 transactions uncovered distinct non-linear risk signatures across Merchant Category Codes (MCC), physical capture modes, structural missingness, and cross-border geographic jumps.

Causal Feature Engineering & Resolving the "Patient Zero" Blind Spot

Standard retrospective velocity aggregations (1h, 24h, 7d) fail to detect the first fraud of an attack burst ("Patient Zero"), accounting for 80.3% of false negatives in V1. Our Feature Distillation 3.0 introduces zero-leakage first-time-seen flags, lifetime account maximum comparisons, and dormancy shock indices in Polars.

Model Benchmarking & Probability Calibration

Evaluation on the blind out-of-time test partition (May 2026: 325,304 transactions). Isotonic calibration aligned predicted probabilities with empirical class frequencies, achieving an Expected Calibration Error of ~0.0000.

3-Tier Risk Triage Policy: Green, Yellow & Red Zones

Rather than imposing a single rigid threshold, continuous Bayesian optimization establishes dual decision cuts: Green Zone (p < 0.274) auto-authorizes 99.5% of volume; Yellow Zone (0.274 ≤ p < 0.842) routes ~31 cases/day to operational review or step-up authentication; Red Zone (p ≥ 0.842) enforces autonomous hard blocks with 91.5% precision.

Regulatory Transparency, TreeSHAP & Attention Maps

Every decline must provide auditable, mathematical justification for dispute handling. TreeSHAP computes exact log-odds attributions per transaction, highlighting signal gaps between detected true positives and missed frauds.
